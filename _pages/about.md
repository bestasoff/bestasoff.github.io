I am a Member of Technical Staff (Research Engineer) at [Mirage](https://mirage.app/), where I focus on advancing the frontiers of AI-powered video generation and computer vision technologies. My work encompasses large-scale model training and inference optimization, applied research, and working with large-scale clusters.

My research interests focus on generative AI and model optimization for large-scale transformers and visual content.

I hold a Bachelor's degree from the Faculty of Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at [Belarusian State University](https://bsu.by/) and completed the Machine Learning Engineer program at [Yandex School of Data Analysis](https://academy.yandex.com/dataschool/). My work is driven by the potential of machine learning to transform creative industries and enable new forms of human-computer interaction.
